Data collected through the contact form

When you send a message through the form on the Contact page, we collect the data you voluntarily enter: name, email address and message. This data is stored in a database (Cloudflare D1) managed by this website.

The name and email address fields are mandatory: without them, the form cannot be submitted and the request cannot be processed. The message field is optional. The form's consent checkbox is also required to submit the form, because it expresses the consent that constitutes the legal basis for the processing described below: without this consent, the form cannot be submitted.

The sender's IP address is never stored in plain text: it is transformed with a hash function, with a secret value added, before being saved, and is used exclusively to limit the number of submissions from the same address within a short period of time, for the purpose of preventing abuse and spam.

Purposes of processing and legal bases

Data collected through the contact form is processed for distinct purposes, each with its own legal basis:

  • Managing and responding to your request (using your name, email and message, including sending the notification and confirmation emails via Resend, see below): legal basis is consent, given via the form's dedicated checkbox (Art. 6(1)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to info@rigonivittorino.com; withdrawal only takes effect for the future and does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out on the basis of consent before its withdrawal.
  • Preventing abuse and spam (limiting the number of submissions from the same IP address via a hash): legal basis is the Data Controller's legitimate interest in protecting the service from misuse (Art. 6(1)(f)). This processing is separate from the one based on consent and is necessary for the form to function securely.
  • Anti-bot verification via Cloudflare Turnstile: legal basis is legitimate interest in the security of the form (Art. 6(1)(f)) — see the dedicated section below for details.

Third parties and recipients of data collected through the contact form

Cloudflare Turnstile: Cloudflare's anti-spam verification script loads as soon as you open the Contact page, so processing by Cloudflare may begin as soon as the form is displayed, not only when it is submitted. For information on how Cloudflare processes this data, see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Resend: messages sent through the form are transmitted to Resend, an email delivery provider, which uses them to deliver the notification email to this company and the confirmation email to the sender. Resend stores data (including message content, delivery logs and account data) in the United States. The transfer of data outside the European Union/EEA is based on the Standard Contractual Clauses under Regulation (EU) 2016/679, incorporated into Resend's data processing agreement, and on Resend's participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. For more information, see Resend's GDPR notice and its Data Processing Addendum (DPA).

Company email provider: the notification email sent via Resend is ultimately delivered to a company mailbox managed by a third-party email service provider, which processes the content of the message as a further party involved in providing the Data Controller's email service.

Google Maps: the Contact page includes an embedded map provided by Google. Loading the map puts your browser in direct communication with Google's servers, which may process your IP address and other technical data according to its own privacy policy.

Hosting and website security

The entire website, not just the contact form, is hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure, which also provides content delivery and protection against automated traffic and abuse. To provide these services, Cloudflare processes technical connection data (such as the IP address and request headers) for every visit to the site. The legal basis for this processing is the legitimate interest of the Data Controller and of Cloudflare in the security and proper functioning of the website (Art. 6(1)(f)). For more information, see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Data retention

Messages received through the contact form are kept for a maximum of 30 days from the date they were sent, after which they are deleted.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymous traffic statistics (for example, pages visited and the approximate origin of visitors). Collection happens via a small script that runs in the visitor's browser, which sends the data directly to Cloudflare's systems; this service does not use cookies or other local storage technologies and does not track individual users across sessions or websites. For this reason, using this service does not require prior consent via a cookie banner. The legal basis is the Data Controller's legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Cookies and local storage

This website does not install its own cookies. On the homepage only, the browser's sessionStorage (a local storage technology, not a cookie) is used to remember, only for the duration of the browsing session, whether you have already seen the initial loading screen, so it is not shown again during the same visit; it is automatically cleared when the tab or browser is closed. This is a strictly technical storage, necessary for the interface to function, which does not require prior consent.

The third parties listed on this page (Cloudflare, Resend, Google Fonts, the icon library and Google Maps) may set their own cookies or use their own technical identifiers, according to their respective privacy policies, which we invite you to consult.

External resources loaded by the browser

To display the pages, your browser downloads some typefaces (via Google Fonts) and an icon library (via a third-party content delivery network) directly from external providers. Loading these resources involves transmitting your IP address and other technical browser data (such as the user agent) to these providers, according to their respective privacy policies. These are technical resources necessary for the website to display normally; the legal basis is the Data Controller's legitimate interest in the website's correct graphical functioning (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Data subject rights

Where processing is based on consent, you also have the right to withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out on the basis of consent before its withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
